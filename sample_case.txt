A 37-year-old man, construction worker, from Hurghada presented with a 5-day
history of fever of 38.5°C, malaise, body ache, nausea, vomiting, and right
upper abdominal pain. No travel history abroad. Denied taking a
supra-therapeutic dose of paracetamol or any other hepatotoxic drugs or herbs.

Exam: mild icterus in the sclera; mild tenderness on deep palpation in the
epigastrium and right hypochondrium.

A few hours after admission: platelet level low; fibrinogen very low
(0.5 g/L, reference 1.8-3.5 g/L). Hemorrhagic manifestations: hematuria,
melena, gingival bleeding, and epistaxis (most prominent).

Imaging: chest X-ray showed bilateral lower and mid-zone haziness with
obliteration of the costophrenic angles (pleural effusion). Abdominal
ultrasound showed fluid collection; the portal vein could not be visualized.

Next day: D-dimer high; hemoglobin dropped to 10 g/dL with 10.8%
reticulocytosis; positive indirect Coombs test. AST, ALT and LDH all
deranged. Disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC) developed; fresh
frozen plasma given. Oliguria, mental disturbance and severe lactic acidosis
(pH 7.0, lactate 19.4 mmol/L, HCO3- 13.1 mmol/L).

Rapid deterioration: pulse 120/min, BP 100/80 mmHg with a 30 mmHg postural
systolic drop. Transferred to ICU, intubated, given inotropes, fresh frozen
plasma, erythrocytes and corticosteroids.

Later labs: hepatitis A/B/C serology negative; blood cultures no growth;
Leptospira and rickettsial serology negative; Dengue IgM positive.
